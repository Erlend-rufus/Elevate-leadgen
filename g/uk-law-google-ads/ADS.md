## First Meta test (a proposal, not approved: Erlend approves creatives and spend)

One campaign, one ad set, objective Leads, optimise on QualifiedForm, UK, age 30 to 65, Facebook and
Instagram only (no Audience Network), no Advantage+ generated text or images. Three ads, one per angle.
Creative: the case card as an image, numbers large, "Norway" visible.

Budget £35 a day for 21 days (about £735). Count leads in ClickUp per utm_content, not in Meta: the pixel
only fires after consent, so Meta sees a part of them.

Stop rules:
1. After £250: link CTR under 0.5% or CPC over £4 on all three ads → new creatives.
2. After £500: no form in ClickUp → stop.
3. At any time: more than half of the forms are not law firms → stop and tighten.
4. After 21 days: fewer than 3 real fit leads, or no meeting held → stop Meta, move the money to Google
   Search or LinkedIn.
