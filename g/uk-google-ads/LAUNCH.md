# Launch notes · /g/uk-google-ads

Google Ads on a fixed monthly fee for UK service businesses. Built 27 Nov 2026 as the UK copy of the Norwegian
leads.elevatemarketing.no/google-ads/ page (Erlend: "kopiere hele planen ... eksl. flytting ... teste i UK"), on the
code of `/g/uk-law-google-ads` (form engine, endpoint, legal pages). Read that LAUNCH.md for the shared parts.

## Offer
- Fixed fee from £1,000 a month (Erlend, 27 Nov 2026), confirmed on the first call. Never a commission.
- Ad budget at least £2,000 a month, paid to Google (the Norwegian page asks 30,000 kr).
- No minimum term, 30 days' notice. Nothing to prepare for the call.

## Form and routing
Twelve steps. No SRA number, practice areas or capacity. The sector is sent as `specialism` so the payload and the
Zapier contract are unchanged. Not a fit: under £1m and no £2,000 a month. Review: £1m or more and no budget,
turnover not stated, a customer worth under £500. Everything else books.
