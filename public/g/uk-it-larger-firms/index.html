Straight answers

How is this different from an agency on a retainer?

A retainer is paid whether or not anything closes. Our invoice depends on a deal closing, so we only earn when your sales team does.

Who owns the ad accounts?

You do. We work inside them. If we stop, they stay with you.

Who pays for the advertising?

You do, directly to the platforms and from ad accounts you own. We add no markup. Our commission is a separate matter and is only invoiced when a deal closes.

What do you need from us to start?

Access to your ad accounts, your CRM, and about an hour with whoever runs sales. Your sales team needs to be able to follow up within two working days.

Why do you ask for turnover?

Because the arithmetic only works above a certain size. We would rather say no early than start something neither of us can make pay.

Do you guarantee results?

No. We can show you the arithmetic and the method. We cannot promise a number of meetings or deals.

What if we want to stop?

There is no minimum term. You stop, and you keep the accounts and the data.
