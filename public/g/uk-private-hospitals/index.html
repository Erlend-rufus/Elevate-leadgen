Beyond the booking: calls, attendance and what a first consultation is worth.

A booking is not the end of the story. The clinic group reports that 92% of online bookings and 90% of phone bookings are attended, that 46% of calls to the clinics become bookings, and that 35% to 60% of consultations lead to a procedure worth about £4,700 on average. Put those figures against the ad spend and the picture turns from cost into value.

  • 1,8611,861taps on the phone number from ads, 45% more than on the same days of 2025 (1,861 against 1,283)
  • £80£80of ad spend per attended first consultation across the whole account, against an average first consultation of £155
  • 1.9x1.9xthe ad spend returned by first consultations alone, before a single procedure (whole account)
  • 13x13xthe ad spend returned once procedures are counted, leaving out searches for the clinic group's own name, at the lower rate of 35% and about £4,700 a procedure (estimate)

How this is built: 1,455 bookings at 92% attendance is 1,339 attended first consultations. At £155 each that is about £207,000 against £107,628 of ad spend, so first consultations alone return it 1.9 times. If 35% of those consultations lead to a procedure at about £4,700, the whole account returns the ad spend about 22 times over. More than half of the bookings come from people searching for the clinic group by name, and we do not take credit for those. Take them out, and the remaining 703 bookings, on their own £89,613 of spend, return it about 13 times. That is the figure we quote. At 60% it would be higher still. These are revenue figures, not profit, and they leave out what we charge. Phone bookings are in none of them: if 46% of the 1,861 calls booked and 90% attended, add roughly half again.

Who this is for, and who it is not.

The groups we work best with already fill most of their diaries through reputation and referral. What they lack is a steady second door: patients who pay for their own consultation and found the clinic because the right campaign was in front of them.

This is for you if

  • You run a private hospital or clinic group in the UK with £5m or more in turnover and at least two years of trading
  • Patients pay for their own specialist consultations, in areas such as gynaecology, urology, orthopaedics, dermatology or diagnostics
  • An attended first consultation is worth £500 or more to you, including the care that follows
  • Your consultants have room in their diaries for more first consultations
  • Your booking team can respond to a new request within one working day

This is not for you if

  • Nearly all of your consultations are paid by insurers or arrive by referral
  • Your consultations are mainly lower-value, such as physiotherapy or routine GP appointments
  • Your turnover is below £5m, or you have been trading for less than two years
  • Nobody has time to approve ads and pages

We check the details you give after you apply, and we confirm turnover and the self-pay share with you on the call.

Paid when first consultations take place. Our incentives are the same as yours.

No retainer. No setup fee. No minimum term. We invoice a commission on the first consultations that take place from campaigns we run, and on the procedures those patients go on to book within a year of that consultation. Not for clicks, not for enquiries, and not for patients who do not turn up. The rate depends on what a consultation is worth to you, so we agree it before anything runs. It is not published, because it is not the same for two groups.

  • An attended first consultation is a new patient's first appointment with a clinician at one of your sites, and it took place. Follow-up appointments, existing patients, and appointments that were cancelled or missed do not count.
  • A procedure counts when that patient books it within a year of the first consultation and it takes place.
  • You confirm each month how many first consultations took place and which procedures followed, as counts and amounts from your booking system. We do not need patient names, contact details or clinical information.
  • We invoice monthly, after you confirm the figures. Payment terms are 14 days.
  • A consultation that is cancelled or missed is not invoiced. If it is rebooked and then takes place, it is invoiced once.
  • The advertising budget is separate. You pay it to Google and Meta directly, and we agree it with you before anything runs.

Nothing runs without your sign-off.

  • You approve every ad

    A named clinician or your head of compliance signs off every ad and every page before it goes live. Every ad text and asset is approved by you before it runs, including any that Google or Meta propose. We do not advertise prescription-only medicines, or consultations that promote them.

  • We do not need patient details

    We do not need patient names, contact details or clinical information. A count of the first consultations that took place, and of the procedures that followed, is enough.

  • Your accounts, your data

    Campaigns run in ad accounts you own. You pay Google and Meta directly, with no markup from us. If we stop, you keep the accounts and the data.

The first meeting: a 30-minute bookings review

We do not pitch. We sit down with your numbers and work out which specialty has the most headroom.

  1. Before the call

    You tell us your turnover, your main specialties, what an attended first consultation is worth, and roughly what you spend on marketing now.

  2. On the call

    We look at which specialty has the most headroom and what it would take to book more consultations there. We ask what you want to reach.

  3. Within one working day

    You receive a one-page sheet: how many attended first consultations a month it takes to reach that target, and how many bookings that means.

You keep the sheet whether or not we work together. It contains your numbers only. No quote, no commission rate, no promise.

Straight answers

Your example is a Norwegian clinic group. Does it carry over to a UK hospital?
Not directly. The health system and the advertising rules differ in the UK, so the cost per booked consultation will differ too. What carries over is the method: one campaign per door, bookings counted in your analytics rather than in the ad platform, and a weekly comparison against the year before.
Why do you show reception separately?
Reception is people searching for the clinic group by name. They already know it, so counting them with the rest would flatter any agency. We show them on their own line, and we quote the whole account and the account without reception side by side.
Is this a referral arrangement?
No. We advertise your services. You decide which patients to see, and we do not choose or recommend a clinician. What we charge is set out in a written agreement that you can show your regulator.
Can you promise a number of bookings?
No. Before we have seen your accounts, any number would be a guess. We can show you how we count and what a real account looked like, and we agree a starting target with you in writing.
What do you need from us?
Access to your ad accounts, a monthly count of the first consultations that came from the campaigns and took place and of the procedures that followed (no patient details), sign-off from a named clinician or your head of compliance on every ad, and a booking team that answers within one working day.
What if we want to stop?
There is no minimum term. You can stop with 30 days' notice, and you keep the accounts and the data.
