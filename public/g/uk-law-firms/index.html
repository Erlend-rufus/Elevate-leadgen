One practice area at a time, tracked to instructions.

A firm that wants more family work does not need more traffic. It needs the people who are searching for a family solicitor this week, a page that answers what they are asking, and a fee earner who calls back the same day. That is the whole mechanism, and it is the same in Bergen and Birmingham. What differs is regulation, competition and cost per click.

  • A clear practice area

    We start with one area where you have capacity and a named fee earner. Campaigns, pages and the follow-up are written for that work and nothing else.

  • Enquiries your fee earners want

    Every enquiry is checked against the criteria we agree in advance before it reaches your inbox. Volume is not the aim. Instructions are.

  • Counted in your own records

    You tell us each month which enquiries became instructions, from your own case management system. That count is what we are judged on and what we invoice on.

For firms that are growing, not firms that are stuck.

The firms we work best with already win work through reputation and referral. What they lack is a second channel they control: enquiries that arrive because the right page was in front of the right person, in a practice area they want to grow.

This works if

  • You are a firm in England and Wales with £2m or more in turnover and at least two years of trading
  • You act for private clients or businesses in areas such as family, conveyancing, wills and probate, employment, commercial or immigration
  • A new client is worth £2,000 or more to you in the first year
  • Someone at the firm can respond to a new enquiry within one working day

This does not work if

  • Your work is mainly personal injury
  • Your work is mainly legal aid
  • You are below £2m turnover or newer than two years
  • Nobody has time to approve marketing material

We check the details you give after you apply, and we confirm turnover with you on the call.

Paid when a new client instructs you. Our incentives are the same as yours.

No retainer, no setup fee, no minimum term. We invoice a commission on new instructions that come from campaigns we run. The rate depends on what a client is worth to you, so we agree it before anything runs, in writing, so you can show the terms to your regulator and, where the rules require it, to your clients. It is not published, because it is not the same for two firms.

  • A new instruction is a new client who has formally instructed the firm, from an enquiry that came from a campaign we run. Existing clients and clients from other sources do not count. Personal injury matters never count.
  • The firm confirms each month which new instructions came from our campaigns, from its own case management records. We need a count and the agreed basis for the commission, not client names or matter details.
  • We invoice monthly, for instructions confirmed that month. Payment terms are 14 days.
  • If nothing is instructed, we invoice nothing.
  • The advertising budget is separate. You pay it to the platforms directly, and we agree it with you before anything runs.

Nothing runs without your sign-off.

  • You approve every word

    A named solicitor at your firm signs off every ad and every page before it goes live. Text and images that the platforms generate on their own are switched off.

  • We never see client matters

    We work with enquiry numbers and outcomes. We never ask for, receive or store anything about a client's matter.

  • Your accounts, your data

    Campaigns run in ad accounts you own, tracked in your own systems. If we stop, you keep everything.

The first meeting: a 30 minute instruction review

We do not pitch. We sit down with your numbers and work out which practice area has the most headroom.

  1. Before the call

    You tell us your turnover, your main practice areas, your growth target for next year, and what a typical new instruction is worth.

  2. On the call

    We look at which practice area has the most headroom and what it would take to bring in more of that work.

  3. Within one working day

    You receive a one page sheet: new instructions per month needed to reach your target, and the enquiries that requires.

You keep the sheet whether or not we work together. It contains your numbers only. No quote, no commission rate, no promise.

Straight answers

Your experience is Norwegian. Why would this work here?
The mechanism is the same: a clear practice area, a clear offer, and enquiries your fee earners want to take. What differs is regulation, competition and cost per click, and we go through those on the first call.
Is this a referral arrangement?
No. We advertise your firm. You decide which clients to take on, and we do not choose or recommend a solicitor. What we charge is set out in a written agreement that you can show your regulator.
Who approves the ads?
A named solicitor at your firm. Nothing runs without that sign-off.
Do you guarantee results?
No. We can show you what we have done and how. We cannot promise a number of enquiries or instructions.
What do you need from us?
Access to your ad accounts, a way to tell which enquiries become instructions (if you do not have one yet, we set one up with you), and about an hour with whoever runs the practice area.
What if we want to stop?
There is no minimum term. You can stop with 30 days' notice, and you keep the accounts and the data.
