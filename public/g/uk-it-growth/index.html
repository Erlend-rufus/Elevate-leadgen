This is for firms that are growing, not firms that are stuck.

The firms we work best with already win work through referrals and reputation. What they lack is a second channel that brings in the same kind of client, predictably, without the partners doing all the selling.

This works if

  • You run a UK IT services firm with £2m or more in turnover and at least two years of trading
  • A new client is worth £25,000 or more to you in the first year
  • You have a sales function, even if that is a founder, that can respond within two working days
  • You have capacity to take on new clients in the next three months

This does not work if

  • You are below £2m turnover or newer than two years
  • You sell products or software licences rather than services
  • You need leads to get through the next quarter
  • You want leads without anyone to follow them up

We check the details you give after you apply, and we confirm turnover with you on the call.

Paid when a deal closes. Our incentives are the same as yours.

No retainer, no setup fee, no minimum term. We invoice a commission on deals that close from campaigns we run. The rate depends on what a client is worth to you, so we agree it before anything runs. It is not published, because it is not the same for two firms.

  • A sale is a signed contract or purchase order with a new client that came from a lead our campaigns produced. Existing clients, and clients that came from elsewhere, do not count.
  • Your own CRM is the record. Each month you confirm which closed deals came from our campaigns, and we invoice only those.
  • We invoice monthly, for deals that closed that month. Payment terms are 14 days.
  • If nothing closes, we invoice nothing.
  • The advertising budget is separate. You pay it to the platforms directly, and we agree it with you before anything runs.

Your accounts. Your data. Your sales team.

The first meeting: a 30 minute pipeline review

We do not pitch. We sit down with your numbers and work out what it would take to reach next year's target.

  1. Before the call

    You tell us your turnover, your target for next year, and what a typical new client is worth.

  2. On the call

    We confirm four numbers together: last year's turnover, next year's target, what a new client is worth in year one, and how many sales meetings turn into clients.

  3. Within one working day

    You receive a one page sheet with the arithmetic: new clients per month needed, sales meetings per month that requires, and how that compares with your pipeline today.

You keep the sheet whether or not we work together. It contains your numbers only. No quote, no commission rate, no promise.

Straight answers

Will this work at our size?
The mechanism is the same: a clear offer, a clear buyer, and campaigns tracked to closed deals. What changes with size is the budget and the sales cycle, and we plan for both on the first call.
How is this different from an agency on a retainer?
A retainer is paid whether or not anything closes. Our invoice depends on a deal closing, so we only earn when your sales team does.
Who owns the ad accounts?
You do. We work inside them. If we stop, they stay with you.
Do you guarantee results?
No. We can show you what we have done, the arithmetic and the method. We cannot promise a number of meetings or deals.
What if we want to stop?
There is no minimum term. You can stop with 30 days' notice, and you keep the accounts and the data.
