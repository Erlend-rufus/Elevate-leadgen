Straight answers

Is this just SEO with a new name?
Partly. AI assistants read the same web as Google, so a site that is easy to read and well referenced helps both. What is new is that an AI answer names a few companies instead of listing ten, leans heavily on third-party sources, and now carries ads. We work on all three.
How soon will we see anything?
ChatGPT ads can bring visits in the first week. Being named in unpaid AI answers usually takes two to four months of content and mentions, and depends on how strong your competitors are.
Do you guarantee results?
No. An AI answer changes from one person and one day to the next, so nobody can honestly promise a position. We track the same questions every month, so you can see whether it is working.
Your case is a Norwegian clinic group. Why would this work for us?
ChatGPT, Gemini and Perplexity give UK and Norwegian buyers the same kind of answer, built from the same kind of sources. What differs is competition and cost per click, and we go through both on the first call.
Can law firms use this?
Yes, for the search work. OpenAI does not take ads for legal services in ChatGPT, so we agree the scope for a law firm on the call.
What if we want to stop?
Give 30 days' notice by email. You keep the content and the ad account.
