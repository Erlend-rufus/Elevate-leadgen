01 How it works

How an assistant decides whose name to say

Worth two minutes before the findings, because every gap below is a failure at one of these four steps.

  1. 1

    A buyer asks a real question

    Not a keyword but a full sentence. The assistant has to answer it with specific names.

  2. 2

    It goes looking for sources

    It does not copy Google’s rankings. It fetches pages it can read, then cross-checks them.

  3. 3

    It reads your site, and what others say about you

    Your own pages say what you do. Registers, directories and press decide whether it believes you.

  4. 4

    It names the firms it can verify

    Verifiable is safe to recommend. Unverifiable is a risk, so it names someone else instead.

That is the entire mechanism, and it is why GEO is two jobs rather than one: make your own pages readable and specific, and build enough independent evidence around them that a machine is willing to put your name in an answer. Nothing here is a trick, and there is no placement to buy.

04 Gaps

Four reasons category answers skip you

Each of these is tied to something observed above, not to general best practice.

1

No verifiable legal identity

The site shows no company number, and we could not locate a Companies House record under the SMCPS name. It may well exist under another registered name, but a machine cannot make that connection, and machines now do the first pass of due diligence.

2

Entity markup stops halfway

The site carries ProfessionalService markup, which is more than most firms we audit. What it lacks is a full Organization and founder graph with a company number and sameAs links. That is precisely what would separate SMCPS from CPS by machine rather than by footnote.

3

Absent from the source pool

The three category answers were assembled from 17, 12 and 13 sites. The visible ones included marra.co.uk, Intelogy, cps.co.uk, Bridgeall, Silicon Reef, Wavenet, Synapx and threesixfive DGTL. SMCPS appears in none of them, so there is nothing for the engine to lift.

4

No independent evidence layer

Your brand answer cited seven sites: your own domain, your LinkedIn page and your own support pages. Accurate, because you wrote it. There is nothing independent underneath it.

Already working for you: an accurate and detailed brand answer with no factual errors, a clear page title and meta description, ProfessionalService markup already in place, and a named proprietary tool, Scenarios + ASCEND, that Google repeats without being asked.

05 The work

Ordered by effect against effort. The left half is the work, the right half is what a machine can do afterwards that it cannot do today.

4

Enter the source pool

Profiles and listings in the directories and partner lists these answers actually cite.

You appear where answers are assembled, not only on your own site.

5

The same four queries re-run after the recrawl window, four to six weeks out.

You see movement in screenshots, not in promises.

We do not promise rankings, mentions or timelines. AI answers vary between runs. What we change is what the systems read, and a repeat measurement shows what moved.

08 Method

How this was measured

All queries were run manually in a browser on 12 August 2026, in English, phrased the way a real buyer would ask rather than as keywords.

Engine: Google AI Mode with UK region and language settings. Four queries in total, three category and one brand test. ChatGPT, Perplexity and Copilot were not run in this round.

Queries were run from Norway with UK language and region settings, so answers to region-specific questions may differ slightly for a buyer physically in the UK.

One answer is one data point, not a verdict. Repeating these queries will produce some variation, which is why the re-measurement uses the same wording.

Website facts were taken from the live pages at smcpsconsulting.co.uk, including page source for structured data. Register checks were made directly against Companies House the same day rather than taken from any AI answer.
