What we actually do

  1. We take the searches an employer runs when choosing a recruiter in your specialism, such as "best fintech recruitment agency in London".
  2. We put them through Google AI Mode and ChatGPT and record which firms get named.
  3. We trace where those answers came from: REC and APSCo listings, Companies House, sector directories, and the firms' own websites.
  4. We show you the gap between what those sources say about you and what they say about the firms getting named.

A shortlist you never see

Recruitment has always run on relationships, and it still does. But a growing share of first contact now starts somewhere else: an employer types a question into an AI and gets back a handful of firms by name.

If your firm is not among them, nothing happens. There is no bounced email and no lost pitch. You simply never hear about the brief. That is the part worth 20 minutes of your time.

What you get on the call

  • A written copy of everything after the call
