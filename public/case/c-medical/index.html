What this case does not show

Read these before you believe the rest.

  1. Attendance. Every figure is an online booking, not a patient in a chair. The 92% attendance rate is the clinic group's own figure.
  2. That we caused all of it. The whole website is up 45% on bookings. Direct visits, which nobody buys, also booked more: 440 against 290. Some of the rise is the clinic group's own reputation and a better website, which we built, and some is demand we did not create.
  3. That a UK clinic gets the same prices. This is a Norwegian account in Norwegian kroner. UK click prices in private healthcare are different, and usually higher.
  4. Searches for the clinic's own name. Over half of the Google Ads bookings come from people who already knew C-Medical. We ran those campaigns, but we do not take credit for that demand. Without reception, bookings are up 15% and the cost per booking down 7%, not 28% and 13%.
  5. The outpatient floor went backwards. Bookings for orthopaedics, dermatology and the other outpatient areas fell 29% once Performance Max was switched off. That was a choice: the campaign was buying brand searches and cheap, poorly qualified bookings.
  6. The spring. From February to mid-May, the rebuilt account booked fewer patients than the old one had a year earlier. A rebuild has a learning period, and this one lasted about fourteen weeks.
  7. Forty-two days are missing. A tracking fault from 13 July to 23 August 2026 hid bookings. Those days are removed from both years, so the comparison is fair, but the 2026 total is lower than the clinic group actually took.

Source and method

How the figures were counted

  1. Source: C-Medical's Google Ads account and Google Analytics 4 property, read through Windsor.ai on 5 November 2026. Published with the clinic group's written permission.
  2. Window: 1 February to 5 November 2026 against the same calendar days of 2025. The 42 days of the tracking fault, 13 July to 23 August, are removed from both years, leaving 236 counted days.
  3. Booking: the GA4 event booking_completed, a completed online booking. Google Ads bookings are those with source and medium google / cpc. Organic is any medium organic. AI search is chatgpt.com, perplexity, copilot, gemini.google.com and claude.ai. Google Ads' own conversion count is not used, because it counts some actions twice.
  4. Treatment areas are derived from campaign names and have not been confirmed by the clinic group.
  5. Currency: NOK converted at £1 = NOK 12.5148, the published rate for 5 November 2026. Kroner are the figures; pounds are for reading.
  6. Revenue multiples use the clinic group's stated attendance (92%), first-consultation price (NOK 1,950) and average procedure value (NOK 59,000), and its stated range of 35% to 60% of consultations leading to a procedure.
