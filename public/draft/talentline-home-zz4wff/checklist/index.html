The question this page is built to answer, quoted from the audit "Which recruitment agencies specialise in financial crime and compliance hiring in the UK?"
What is weak about the current site The web presence is a single portal page carrying the page title "Talentline Portal", so neither a buyer nor a search engine can tell what the firm specialises in.
Page title to set Talentline Recruitment | Financial crime, compliance and senior finance search, UK and Gulf
The one to settle first The founder's registered name. Google lists Saqib "Zac" Hussain, the enquiry to us came from Saqib Awan. On a page whose whole argument is that you can verify who you are dealing with, we do not choose between them. The client confirms it in the meeting.
