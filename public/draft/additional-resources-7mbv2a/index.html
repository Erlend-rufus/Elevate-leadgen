What we do for employers

We recruit permanent, contract and interim staff for businesses that do not have an internal recruitment function. That means we take the brief, write the advert, screen the applications, run the first interviews and hand you a shortlist you can actually work with. For a small business the practical value is that you get one supplier covering several different types of role, instead of a separate agency for each department. Fee basis and rebate terms are [[TO CONFIRM: terms of business, fee basis and rebate period]].

How the Associate Partner model works for you

Your vacancy is handled by an Associate Partner: an experienced recruiter who runs their own specialist desk inside Additional Resources. This is the part of the model worth understanding, because it is the reason a business of our size can cover as many sectors as it does.

From your side it has three consequences. You deal directly with the person doing the work. That person has chosen to specialise in your sector, so the market knowledge is genuine rather than researched the week you called. And the commercial side stays central: one agreement with Additional Resources, one invoice, one place to escalate if something goes wrong.
