The question this page is built to answer, quoted from the audit "Which UK IT support companies are best for a small business that needs Cyber Essentials certification?"
What is weak about the current site The site is built in the MyWebsite NOW template tool, carries the spelling errors "beacuse" and "buisness" in the body copy, and its social links point to the Facebook and Instagram home pages rather than to Rounded IT's own profiles.
On the accreditations band The five scheme names are drawn because Google rewards them in this category. Whether Rounded IT holds any of them is the first question on Monday. If the answer is no for some, the band shows fewer schemes, or shows them with an in-progress status. It never shows a scheme we cannot evidence.
Colour and type A direction, not finished branding. No logo and no part of the existing visual identity has been reused. Nothing on the page carries a number, a client name, a rating or a quotation.
