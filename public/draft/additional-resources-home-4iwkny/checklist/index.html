The question this page is built to answer, quoted from the audit "Which UK recruitment agencies can handle permanent hiring across several different sectors for a small business?"
What is weak about the current site The front page opens with "Looking for a job?", while the questions that bring buyers to this category come from employers.
Three facts Google publishes that the client has never told us Established 1999, an address in Brentwood in Essex, and FCSA Recruiter Partner status. All three are drawn as placeholders rather than printed, because we have not heard any of them from the company.
The one to press on What Castleford, Sheffield, Doncaster and Market Weighton actually are. Four town names set that large implies four offices. If they are coverage areas the page has to say so.
