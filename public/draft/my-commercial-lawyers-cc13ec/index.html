What we handle

Three kinds of dispute, one way of working

Facts, contract and evidence first. The aim is not to push every matter towards legal action, but to tell you whether to negotiate, adjudicate, litigate or take another step.

01

Construction disputes

Payment, delay, defect and contract disputes under JCT, NEC and FIDIC, from unpaid invoices to adjudication.

02

Commercial and contract disputes

Breach of contract, business debt recovery and other contentious commercial matters where there is a clear commercial reason to proceed.

03

Litigation and arbitration

Representation where suitable in the County Court, the High Court and the Technology and Construction Court, and in cross-border arbitration.
