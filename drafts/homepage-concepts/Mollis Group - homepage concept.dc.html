Canvas note · not part of the page Mollis Group, homepage concept
The question this page is built to answer, quoted from the audit Verbatim audit question to be pasted in The brief did not include it for this client. The page is built on the sector split, so the question is most likely a finance or construction phrasing of managed IT.
What is weak about the current site The site is WordPress with the Bridge theme and WPBakery, text heavy and without hierarchy, and it carries an animal metaphor throughout that sits awkwardly against the conservation commitment.
On the two numbers The current site claims fifteen years of experience while the founder is listed from 2019. Neither figure appears anywhere on this page. Both become one placeholder in the footer, and the client tells us which is right.
The argument to make in the meeting The content on their site is largely fine. What it lacks is air, hierarchy and one clear entry per sector. This page is the same substance with those three things added, which makes it the easiest of the five to argue for.
